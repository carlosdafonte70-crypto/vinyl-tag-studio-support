PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This privacy information describes Vinyl Tag Studio, a macOS music metadata editor by Carlos Dafonte Fernandez, and this GitHub Pages support website.

Files processed by the Mac app

Vinyl Tag Studio works on the audio files and folders that you select on your Mac. Its music editing features do not require a cloud account. Metadata edits and artwork changes are performed locally, and preferences, access permissions, and original-file backups may be stored on your device.

Visiting this website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with website delivery, including IP addresses and access logs, as explained in GitHub's privacy policies. This site does not include third-party advertising pixels or its own analytics scripts.

Contacting support

Support requests are currently handled through GitHub Issues. A GitHub account is required to post an issue. Issues are public, so never include passwords, confidential audio files, personal contact details, or other sensitive information. The information you submit to GitHub is processed according to GitHub's terms and privacy policies.

Deleting submitted information

If you need an issue corrected or removed, you can request help through the issue tracker. You can also manage your GitHub account's data and privacy options directly with GitHub.

Contact

For app-support and privacy questions, use the support issue tracker. Support requests may remain visible until resolved and may be retained under GitHub's content and retention policies.
